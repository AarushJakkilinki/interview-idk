An interview site where you can practice for interviews by talking to an AI. There are many different tech jobs to select from, and you can also upload your resume for grading and suggestions, which can also be used during the interview. There is a live translation of the interview, also containing your WPM and filler words. The interview questions adapt to whatever role you select. After the interview, there is a fully detailed grader that tells where you did wrong and where you performed strongly, while also giving suggestions and other tips. Made by gemini.
